Publishing to Play Store & App Store

Production App Store Bundle (.aab), Apple iOS (.ipa) & Global Cloud

Google Play Store (Android) Standard .aab

Google Play strictly forbids raw .apk uploads for new apps. It requires an .aab (Android App Bundle). Capacitor generates this native Android Studio project directly. Once uploaded to Google Play Console, anyone can install Trip Barabar with 1 tap from Play Store search!

Apple App Store (iPhone & iPad) Xcode Archive

Capacitor builds a 100% native iOS Xcode project (npx cap add ios). You open Xcode on macOS, select your Apple Developer account, and upload the build directly to TestFlight and the official Apple App Store.

Real-Time Cloud Sync (No Local Server Needed) Global 24/7

For users worldwide to use the app when your PC is turned off: The frontend is hosted on Cloudflare Pages or Firebase (free high-speed global CDN), connected to Supabase or Firestore. When Ankuj logs an expense, all 8 friends receive instant push updates over WebSockets!

⚡ Instant Web Join (No App Install Required)

For friends who don't want to download any app from app stores, they can simply tap your trip link (e.g. tripbarabar.com/#join=THAI26) and it opens immediately in Safari/Chrome with full access!
